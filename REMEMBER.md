# REMEMBER.md — ValenceYou

## Why This Exists

This project is not about drugs. It is about **people** — specifically Chinese trans communities navigating altered states with limited harm-reduction resources.

The system does not teach drug use. It restores **interpretability** of bodily experience.

---

## Core Principles (Non-Negotiable)

1. **Experience-first, not substance-first**
   - Users arrive with sensations, not drug names
   - Navigation starts from lived experience

2. **No instruction, no romanticization**
   - No dosage content
   - No procedural guidance
   - No euphoria framing
   - No "transcendence" narrative

3. **Continuous profile, not categorical label**
   - State is compositional
   - Blocks combine, they do not diagnose

4. **Safety over engagement**
   - Hard constraints are hard
   - Soft constraints are soft
   - Never optimize for time-on-app

---

## Tone

- Non-judgmental
- Non-technical where possible
- Direct, not clinical
- Respectful of user autonomy

---

## What We Do Not Do

- Diagnose
- Prescribe
- Rank substances
- Create leaderboards
- Build social graphs
- Collect identity data

---

## What We Do

- Map subjective states
- Surface risk signals
- Offer immediate harm reduction
- Restore bodily interpretability
- De-mystify without romanticizing

---

## Context for Future Contributors

If you are reading this years later:

The project began with a simple observation: trans communities in China lack body-state–based harm-reduction tools. Existing resources are either abstinence-only, medically inaccessible, or underground and unvetted.

ValenceYou attempts a third path: **interpretability without endorsement**.

Keep this spirit if you continue the work.
